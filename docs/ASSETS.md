# Bundled assets

The story is original sample prose created for this MVP. Illustrations were generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-11-12 and compressed to JPEG for offline use. No image API is used by the app.

- `assets/scenes/breakwater.jpg`: tiny white-stone lighthouse at the end of a curved Atlantic breakwater at blue hour; woman in a mustard raincoat carrying a brass lantern; mist, teal waves, distant cliffs; literary gouache, textured paper, muted blue, teal, silver and gold; landscape 3:2, no text.
- `assets/scenes/lantern-room.jpg`: inside a coastal lighthouse lantern room; brown-haired woman in a mustard raincoat beside a brass Fresnel lens; notebook and lantern on a wooden ledge; first golden sunrise over the sea and distant village; matching textured gouache, landscape 3:2, no text.
- `assets/reader-icon.png`: project-created geometric book and sun icon.
- `assets/fonts/Literata.ttf`: Literata variable font from Google Fonts, licensed under the bundled `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`.
