# Bundled assets

The story is original sample prose created for this MVP. Illustrations were generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-11-12 and compressed to JPEG for offline use. No image API is used by the app.

## Scene 1

Saved asset: `assets/scenes/breakwater.jpg`.

Final generation prompt:

> Create a beautiful literary book illustration, landscape 3:2 format. A tiny solitary white stone lighthouse with a dark navy lantern roof stands at the end of a long curved stone breakwater on the Atlantic coast, viewed from shore at blue hour just before sunrise. The lighthouse lantern glows amber. A small adult woman in a mustard yellow raincoat, seen from behind, walks toward it carrying a small brass lantern. Sea mist, quiet teal waves, distant navy cliffs. Atmospheric painterly gouache, fine grain of paper, sophisticated illustrated novel, restrained blue teal silver and gold palette, rich detail and tactile brushwork, evocative and quiet, cinematic composition. No text, no lettering, no borders. This is the first illustration for an original story called The Light Between Tides.

## Scene 2

Saved asset: `assets/scenes/lantern-room.jpg`.

Final generation prompt:

> Create a literary illustrated-novel scene, landscape 3:2 format. Inside the lantern room at the top of a small old white-stone coastal lighthouse, a young adult woman with brown hair in a mustard yellow raincoat stands seen from behind by the huge brass Fresnel lens. A small open weathered notebook and brass hand lantern lie on a wooden ledge. Through tall windows the vast calm Atlantic sea catches the very first golden light of sunrise, silver-blue horizon and distant coastal village. Atmospheric painterly gouache with textured paper and fine detailed brushwork, restrained muted teal, slate blue, pale gold, evocative stillness, elegant cinematic composition. Match the aesthetic of a sophisticated storybook coastal lighthouse illustration. No text, no lettering, no borders.
